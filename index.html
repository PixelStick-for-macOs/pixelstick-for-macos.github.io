How It Works

1. Launch and Position

Open PixelStick. Drag endpoints to your target on screen—any app, any window.

2. Measure Distance or Angle

Stretch horizontally, vertically, or diagonally. Click locks to constrain movement. Values update in real-time.

3. Pick Colors

Activate eyedropper. Click any pixel. Color copies to clipboard in your preferred format.

4. Scale and Export

Apply built-in or custom scaling. Readings stay precise for maps, designs, or edits.
